About these figures

A return counts as soon as it reaches the KoboToolbox server.  The page counts people who agreed to take part (A1); those who declined are shown separately.  Returns marked “Not approved” in KoboToolbox, such as tests, are left out.

There is no list of the people the survey went to, so these are counts of respondents, not a response rate.  Several people from one office may answer, and nothing on this page tells them apart.

What this page does not show

It shows counts only.  It holds no names, telephone numbers or email addresses, no comments or other typed text, and none of the answers beyond the profile questions in section B, the systems named in D2 and the two follow-up offers.  The findings of the survey come from the analysis, not from this page.

Counts in a small group can still point to a person.  Please keep the password within the assessment team and do not copy small counts into documents that go outside it.
